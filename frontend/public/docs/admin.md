# Admin Guide

The Admin role is responsible for the day-to-day operations and configuration of a specific school branch.

## Academic Setup

Configure the academic structure of your school.

### Classes and Sections
Create classes (e.g., Grade 1, Grade 2) and their respective sections (e.g., 1A, 1B).

### Subjects
Define the subjects taught in the school and assign them to specific classes.

## Staff Management

Manage teaching and non-teaching staff.

### Add Staff
Register new staff members, providing their personal, contact, and professional details.

### Assign Roles
Assign appropriate roles (Teacher, Accounts, etc.) to staff members.

## Student Management

Oversee student records and admissions.

### Admissions
Process new student admissions, capturing necessary information and documents.

### Student Records
View and update existing student profiles, including academic history and attendance.

## Communication

Send notices, alerts, and messages to staff, students, and parents.
