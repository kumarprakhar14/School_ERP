# Welcome to the School ERP User Guide

This guide will help you understand and navigate the different modules of the School ERP system.

## Overview

The School ERP system is designed to streamline administrative and academic processes. Depending on your role, you will have access to different modules and functionalities.

## Getting Started

1. **Login**: Use your provided credentials to log in.
2. **Dashboard**: Upon login, you will see a dashboard tailored to your role.
3. **Navigation**: Use the sidebar to navigate through the available modules.

Select a module from the left sidebar to learn more about its specific features and capabilities.
