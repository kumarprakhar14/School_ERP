# Welcome to the School ERP User Guide

School ERP is an all-in-one school management platform that helps administrators, teachers, students, and accounts staff manage their daily school operations from a single place — with no paperwork and no confusion.

This guide covers everything you need to know to use the application confidently, from your very first login to day-to-day operations. Pick the section from the left that matches your role to get started.

---

## What is School ERP?

School ERP is a role-based platform. This means that what you see and what you can do in the application depends entirely on your role. There are five roles in the system:

- **Super Admin** — The platform owner who manages all schools registered on the system.
- **Admin** — The school administrator who runs day-to-day operations of a specific school.
- **Teacher** — Teaching staff who mark attendance, manage assignments, and post notices.
- **Student** — Students who can view their timetable, assignments, fees, and notices.
- **Accounts** — Finance staff who manage fee invoices and record payments.

---

## How Do I Log In?

1. Open the application in your browser and you will see the **Login** page.
2. Enter the **ERP ID** and **Password** given to you by your school administrator or the platform team.
3. Click **Login**. You will be taken directly to your role-specific dashboard.

> If you forget your password or do not have credentials, please contact your school administrator.

---

## Navigating the Application

Once logged in, you will see a **sidebar** on the left side of the screen. This is your main navigation menu. Every section in the sidebar is a module of the application. Click on any item to open that module.

The sidebar also shows your **school's name and logo** at the top, so you always know which school's data you are viewing.

At the bottom of the sidebar, you will find two helpful items:
- **Report an Issue** — Use this to report any bugs or problems you notice in the app.
- **User Guide** — Opens this guide, which you are currently reading.

---

## Your Profile

You can access your profile by clicking your name or avatar in the **top navigation bar**. From there you can view your account details, update your contact information, and change your profile picture.

---

## Select a guide from the left sidebar

Each guide on the left explains exactly what that role can do — screen by screen, feature by feature. Select the guide that matches your role to continue.
