# Accounts Guide

The Accounts role is responsible for managing all financial records for the school — generating fee invoices for students, recording payments, and keeping an eye on the overall fee collection status.

You do not manage academic records or user accounts. Your focus is entirely on fees.

---

## Your Sidebar at a Glance

| Sidebar Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | School notice board and overall announcements |
| **Fees** | Generate invoices, record payments, and view fee summaries |

---

## Dashboard

Your Dashboard shows the school's **Notice Board** — all active announcements published by the Admin or teachers. This keeps you informed of any school-wide updates or important dates.

---

## Fees

The Fees page is your primary workspace. It covers everything related to student fee management.

### Fee Summary

When you open the Fees page, you see a **summary table** showing all students in the school with their fee status:

- **Student Name & ERP ID** — who the record belongs to.
- **Class/Section** — the student's current class.
- **Total Amount** — total fee charged to the student.
- **Amount Paid** — how much has been received so far.
- **Due Amount** — the remaining outstanding balance.
- **Status** — the current fee status:
  - **Paid** — all invoices have been fully paid.
  - **Pending** — an invoice exists but no payment has been recorded yet.
  - **Partially Paid** — some payment received, but balance remains.
  - **Overdue** — unpaid balance with a due date that has already passed.

You can use this summary to quickly identify which students have outstanding dues.

### Generating a Fee Invoice

To charge a student's account for a particular month:

1. Click **Create Invoice**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Student** — Search for and select the student.
   - **Amount** — Enter the fee amount.
   - **Month** — The month the fee is for (1–12).
   - **Year** — The year the fee is for.
   - **Due Date** *(optional)* — Set a payment deadline.
   - **Remarks** *(optional)* — Any notes (e.g., "June Tuition Fee").
3. Click **Create**. An invoice number is automatically generated by the system (e.g., `INV-2026-6-XXXXX`).

> Each invoice is specific to a month and year. You can create multiple invoices for the same student for different months.

### Recording a Payment

Once you receive a fee payment from a student, record it here:

1. Click **Record Payment**.
2. Fill in the payment details:
   - **Invoice** — Select the invoice this payment is against.
   - **Amount** — The amount received.
   - **Payment Mode** *(optional)* — e.g., Cash, Online, Cheque.
   - **Reference Number** *(optional)* — Transaction ID or cheque number.
   - **Remarks** *(optional)* — Any notes.
3. Click **Record Payment**. The payment is logged immediately and the student's fee status updates accordingly.

### Transaction History

Below the summary, you can view a chronological log of all invoices and payments across the school. Each entry is labeled as either an **Invoice** or a **Payment**, along with the amount, date, and student details.

You can filter the history by a specific student to see only their records.

> If you need to bulk-import fees from a spreadsheet (e.g., for a new academic year), ask your school Admin to use the **Data Import** feature in their panel.
