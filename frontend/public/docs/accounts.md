# Accounts Guide

The Accounts role manages the financial aspects of the school, including fee collection and expense tracking.

## Fee Management

Streamline the fee collection process.

### Fee Structures
Define different fee types (Tuition, Transport, Library, etc.) and create fee structures for various classes.

### Fee Collection
Process fee payments from students, generate receipts, and track outstanding balances.

## Expense Management

Record and categorize school expenses.

### Track Expenses
Log daily expenses, upload bills, and categorize them for better financial reporting.

## Reporting

Generate comprehensive financial reports.

### Daily Collection Report
View a summary of all fees collected on a specific date.

### Outstanding Fees Report
Identify students with pending fee payments to follow up accordingly.
