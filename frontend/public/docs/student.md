# Student Guide

As a Student, the application is your personal school portal. You can check your timetable, view and submit assignments, see your fees, and stay updated with notices from your school — all from one place.

---

## Your Sidebar at a Glance

| Sidebar Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | See today's attendance status, pending assignments, and school notices |
| **Academics** | View the subjects you study and their assigned teachers |
| **Time Table** | View your class schedule for the week |
| **Assignments** | View assignments given to your class and submit your work |
| **Fees** | Check your fee invoices, payment history, and outstanding balance |

---

## Dashboard

Your Dashboard shows the most important information at a glance:

- **My Status** — Two quick cards at the top:
  - **Today's Attendance** — Shows whether you were marked Present, Absent, Leave, or Not Marked yet for today.
  - **Pending Assignments** — Shows how many upcoming assignments you have not submitted yet.
- **Notice Board** — All announcements from your school that are addressed to students (or to everyone) appear here, with the name of the person who posted them and when.

---

## Academics

The Academics page shows all the subjects you are currently enrolled in, along with the teacher(s) assigned to teach each subject.

This is pulled directly from your class timetable, so it always reflects your current academic setup. If no timetable has been set up for your class yet, this page will show an empty message.

---

## Time Table

The Time Table page shows your full weekly class schedule. You can see:
- Which subject you have on which day.
- What period (time slot) each class falls in.
- Which teacher teaches each slot.

The timetable is set up and maintained by your school Admin. You can only view it here — no changes can be made from the student side.

---

## Assignments

The Assignments page shows all assignments that your teacher has posted for your class section.

### What you will see

Each assignment card shows:
- The assignment title and description.
- The due date (deadline for submission).
- Whether you have already submitted or not.
- An attached file from the teacher, if any.

### Submitting an assignment

1. Click on an assignment to open it.
2. Click the **Submit** button.
3. Attach your completed work as a file and confirm.
4. Once submitted, your submission is recorded. You can see it marked as "Submitted" on the assignment card.

> You can only submit once per assignment. Make sure your file is complete before submitting.

## Fees

The Fees page gives you a clear view of your fee records.

### Fee Summary

At the top of the Fees page, you will see a summary of your overall fee status:
- **Total Amount** — the total fee charged to you.
- **Amount Paid** — how much has been paid so far.
- **Due Amount** — the remaining balance.
- **Status** — one of the following:
  - **Paid** — all dues cleared.
  - **Pending** — an invoice was generated but no payment has been received yet.
  - **Partially Paid** — some amount has been paid but a balance remains.
  - **Overdue** — there is an outstanding balance and the due date has passed.
  - **No Fees** — no invoices have been generated for your account yet.

### Transaction History

Below the summary, you will see a detailed chronological list of all your fee invoices and payments. Each entry shows the amount, date, month/year the fee is for, and the payment mode (for payments).

### Viewing and Downloading Invoices & Receipts

Every row in the Transaction History has a **document icon** (📄) on the right. Clicking it opens a formatted PDF document:

- For an **Invoice** row — opens the full fee invoice showing your name, class, invoice number, billing period, amount, balance due, and payment status watermark (PAID / PARTIALLY PAID / PENDING). The invoice includes the school logo and a QR code for authenticity verification.
- For a **Payment** row — opens the payment receipt with the same details plus the payment mode and reference number.

On desktop, the PDF is previewed directly in the browser. On mobile, a **Download PDF** button is provided instead. Both views include a **Download PDF** button to save or print the document.

### Paying via UPI / QR Code

If your school has UPI configured, you can pay your fee directly from the app:

1. On the Fees page, open the invoice you want to pay.
2. Click **Pay via UPI**. A QR code and a UPI deep-link will appear on screen.
3. Scan the QR code with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) or tap the link if you are on mobile.
4. Complete the payment in your UPI app.
5. Come back to the app and click **Submit Payment Proof**.
6. Enter the **UTR number** (the 12-digit reference number shown in your UPI app after a successful payment). You can also optionally attach a screenshot of the payment confirmation.
7. Click **Submit**. Your payment will be recorded as **Pending Verification**.

Once the school's accounts staff or admin reviews and approves your submission, the status will update to confirmed and your fee record will reflect the payment.

> Your fee status will not change to Paid until the payment has been verified by the school. If you believe there is an error or a delay in verification, contact your school's accounts office.

> Fee invoices and payments are managed by your school's Accounts staff. If you believe there is an error in your fee records, please contact your school office.
