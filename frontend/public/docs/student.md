# Student Guide

The Student portal provides access to academic information, schedules, and learning resources.

## Dashboard

Your central hub for important updates, upcoming classes, and pending tasks.

## Academics

Track your academic progress.

### Timetable
View your class schedule to know where you need to be and when.

### Assignments
View assigned homework, project details, and deadlines. Submit your completed work directly through the portal.

### Grades
Check your results for recent exams and assignments.

## Attendance

Monitor your attendance record for the current academic year.

## Resources

Access study materials, lecture notes, and other resources shared by your teachers.
